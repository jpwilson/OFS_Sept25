
SHOTS (all landscape 4:3, all PHOTO, opaque, snapshot look, from the same family's camera roll in February: a kitchen renovation in an ordinary British terraced house). Use sheet A and sheet B as the reference so these are the same people. No readable text, logos or brand marks anywhere, including on packaging and tools. Everyone fully and ordinarily dressed.

1. `photo-kitchen-before.png` — the tired old kitchen before the work, nobody in it: 1990s orange-pine cabinets, beige tiles, a flickery strip light on the ceiling, cluttered worktops with a kettle, fruit bowl and post. Bright daylight from a window, shot from the doorway at chest height.

2. `photo-kitchen-sledge.png` — TOM (sheet A) in safety goggles, old grey tee and work gloves, mid-swing with a sledgehammer at an old orange-pine wall cabinet, plaster dust in the air, grinning. Bright daylight, the half-demolished kitchen around him.

3. `photo-kitchen-camping.png` — MAYA, TOM, IVY and LEO (sheet A, their sheet outfits) sitting on the living-room floor around a small camping stove with a kettle on it, eating pizza out of plain boxes, laughing; sofa behind, dust sheets folded in a corner. BISCUIT, the small scruffy sandy terrier, knee-high, exactly as on sheet A, nosing at a crust. Bright daytime window light.

4. `photo-kitchen-tiling.png` — MAYA (sheet A) kneeling on the floor of the stripped kitchen laying patterned encaustic floor tiles, in a white tee and indigo dungarees with a mustard headscarf, trowel in hand, concentrating with a small smile. Taken from slightly above.

5. `photo-kitchen-paint.png` — IVY and LEO (sheet A) in old oversized shirts over their clothes painting a cabinet door laid flat on newspaper with sage-green paint, brushes in hand, a dab of sage-green paint on Leo's nose, both giggling.

6. `photo-kitchen-done.png` — the finished kitchen: sage-green shaker cabinets, pale oak worktops, white butler sink under the window, open oak shelves with plants and mugs, terrazzo floor, a pendant lamp; MAYA, TOM, IVY and LEO at the small island eating pancakes, Tom flipping one in a pan, everyone laughing. BISCUIT (small scruffy sandy terrier, knee-high, exactly as on sheet A) sitting hopefully by the stools. Bright morning daylight.
